Product safety: manufacturer information and notes

English summary for convenience — the German version is authoritative.

Manufacturer and person responsible under the EU General Product Safety Regulation ((EU) 2023/988, GPSR): Niklas-Amoah Alotey, trading as "Kniff", Württembergallee 27, 14052 Berlin, Germany – kniffshop@gmail.com. See also the Legal Notice.

All items are printed by us in Berlin from PLA (polylactic acid, plant-based) using FDM printing, and hand-checked before shipping.

  • Not food-safe. These parts are not food-safe and not suitable for storing open food.
  • Not heat-resistant. PLA softens from around 50 °C — do not store long-term in direct sun (e.g. behind a windscreen), on radiators, or in the dishwasher.
  • Not a toy. Some items contain small parts — keep away from children under 3 years.
  • Keep away from open flame. PLA is flammable.
  • Stop using an item if it shows visible cracks or brittle material.

Cleaning: by hand with lukewarm water and mild dish soap, no abrasives, not dishwasher-safe.
