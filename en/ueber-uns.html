About Kniff

The first Kniff was a coin sorter.

We both knew the problem: always loose change on you, never the right coin to hand.

More out of curiosity than any plan.

So we built ourselves a sorter: eight tubes, one for each euro coin, the value embossed on top. We listed it on eBay. That was the first part anyone had ever ordered from us, rather than received as a gift.

Workshop in Berlin. Small batches, no assembly line.

Every part goes through our check on its own before it ships. We print pretty much anything a 3D printer can produce, and adapt models on request.
