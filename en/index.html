Before you ask

What does a custom print cost?

It depends on size, material, print time, finishing and quantity. The assessment is free and without obligation.

Do I need a finished 3D file?

No. A sketch, a photo with measurements or a short description is enough to start.

Which materials and colours are available?

Standard is matte black PLA. More than ten other colour and material options are available on request, including pastel shades, metallic and PETG.
