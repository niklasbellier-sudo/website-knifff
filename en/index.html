How it started

The first Kniff was a coin sorter.

We both knew the problem: always loose change on you, never the right coin to hand. A bit in your pocket, a bit in the centre console, the rest in a bowl by the door. We still had to search every single time.

So we built a sorter: eight tubes, one for each euro coin, the value embossed on top. We listed it on eBay, more out of curiosity than any plan. In fourteen days, eighteen sold — all to people we'd never met. That was the first part anyone had ever ordered from us, rather than received as a gift – and the moment it became clear there was more to it than that.

Frequently asked questions

Before you ask.

What does a custom print cost?

That depends on size, material, print time, finishing and quantity. That's why every request gets a personal fixed price instead of a flat rate. The assessment is free and non-binding.

Do I need a finished 3D file?

No. A sketch, a photo with measurements, or a short description is enough to start. If you have a file (STL, 3MF, STEP), it's faster. We can adapt models or design them together with you if needed.

How long does it take?

We usually reply to your request within two business days. Once you approve, print time depends on size and quantity – the exact delivery time is in the quote. Shipping within Germany via DHL, usually 1–2 business days.

What materials and colours are available?

Default is matte black PLA, a plant-based material. Over ten further colour and material variants are available on request, including pastel tones, metallic finishes, and PETG for better heat resistance. We print new colours as a sample first.

Who owns the rights to my design?

You do. You remain the owner of the rights to the file you submit. By submitting it, you confirm you're authorised to use it. We only print other people's copyrighted designs with proven permission.

Can I return it if I don't like it?

That depends on what you ordered.

As a consumer, you can withdraw from an order for ready-made shop products within 14 days without giving a reason. Choosing an offered colour variant doesn't change that.

Parts made individually to your specifications through Custom Print, on the other hand, are legally excluded from the right of withdrawal (§ 312g(2) no. 1 BGB). That's why you get a quote to approve before anything is printed.

If there's a defect or a printing error on our part, the statutory warranty applies either way – just get in touch and we'll find a solution. Details in the Right of Withdrawal page.
