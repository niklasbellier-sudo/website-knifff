Shipping & payment

Shipping & payment.

Shipping within Germany via Deutsche Post or DHL, from €2.90. Payment by card, Apple Pay and Google Pay.

Shipping

Small, flat items such as the sun visor clip and the pen holder are sent as a Deutsche Post large letter (Maxibrief) for €2.90. Letters have no tracking and usually arrive within 3 business days of dispatch. All other items ship as a DHL small parcel or parcel up to 2 kg for approx. €4.19 to €6.19, depending on size and weight; delivery usually takes 1 to 2 business days after dispatch.

We currently ship within Germany.

Because some products are only printed once the order comes in, delivery can take longer in individual cases. This is noted on the respective product page.

Shipping carriers: Deutsche Post (large letter) and DHL (small parcel and parcel, postage paid online).

Payment methods

Credit and debit card, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Payments are processed via Revolut Business.

Packaging

We keep packaging as lean and recyclable as possible. Kniff is registered in the LUCID packaging register.
