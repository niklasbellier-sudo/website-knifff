How it works.

Drop a coin in at the top and it does the rest: it rolls down a sloped rail and falls through the window that fits it exactly. From 1 cent to 2 euros, every coin lands in its own tube.

Each tube shows you when a bank roll is full. Pull it out, slip the coin wrapper over it and take your change to the bank. In testing, every coin type landed in the right tube ten times out of ten. Coins are dropped in one at a time.

Assembled size 28 × 7 × 20 cm. Printed in PLA, black with an orange lid.
