Do I need a finished 3D file?

No. A sketch, a photo with measurements or a short description is enough to start.

Who owns the rights to my design?

You do. You keep the rights to the file you submit.

Can I return it if I don't like it?

Custom-made parts are excluded from the statutory right of withdrawal (§ 312g (2) no. 1 BGB). That's why you approve the quote before anything is printed. If there is a defect, the statutory warranty applies. Details in the withdrawal information.
